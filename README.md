# Policies

These are policies that the Constellation of Kyndness holds true. They are not necessarily rules to be enforced, but are intended to give you an idea of what to expect in how the Constellation of Kyndness is run.



## Nudity isn't inherently sexual

Nudity is considered normal here. While sexual content should be kept to appropriate places/moments as much as reasonable, nudity isn't considered sexual in these communities.
